How it works

New to CTFs? In a jeopardy-style event you pick challenges from a board, solve them in any order and score points for your team.

  1. Form a team and register

    Registration is open on our CTFd instance. Join the Discord to find teammates.

  2. Solve challenges

    Each challenge hides a flag, which is a short piece of text, often like oo{Sample_Flag_Do_Not_Use}. Each challenge tells you the exact format to submit.

  3. Submit flags and score

    Submit the flag on CTFd to earn points for your team. New challenges unlock in waves, so there's always something to come back to.

What you'll solve

Difficulty runs from introductory to advanced, so there's something for every level.

  • Web exploitation

    Find and abuse flaws in websites and web apps.

  • Cryptography

    Break, or properly use, the maths that keeps secrets secret.

  • Forensics

    Dig evidence out of files, disk images and network captures.

  • Reverse engineering

    Work out what a program does without its source code.

  • Binary exploitation

    Find memory bugs in compiled programs and take control of them.

  • OSINT

    Track down answers using publicly available information.

  • AI

    Prompt injection, model exploitation and machine learning security.

  • Miscellaneous

    Everything that doesn't fit in a box.

Who's behind it

Outback Overflow is the first event from the High School Cybersecurity Community, a student-run group for high schoolers across Australia. We're building the challenges with support from university security societies including UNSW SecSoc, UTS Cybersecurity Society, and more.
